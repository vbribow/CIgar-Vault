# Private release phone acceptance

**Release scope:** owner-only Hojavía private collector app.

**Public launch:** held pending trademark clearance.

**Paid research:** disabled; no billing, credential, migration, or activation is authorized.

## Safe setup

- Use the owner-only Sites address on one iPhone and one Android phone.
- Use disposable test records only; do not alter founder inventory.
- Record phone, operating system, browser or installed-app mode, build identifier, date, and pass/fail.
- Stop if unavailable data appears as zero, empty, deleted, verified, safe, or complete.

## Five-minute platinum journey

1. Open Home and confirm **Resume where I left off** names only a workspace—not a cigar or record.
2. Open **My collection**, set a search and filter, load more records, open one test record, then return. Confirm the filter, loaded count, and list position return.
3. Begin an inventory edit, enter disposable details, leave before saving, return, and confirm the owner-scoped draft is restored. Confirm passwords and files are not restored.
4. Open camera documentation, start identification, and confirm progress remains visible. Interrupt the connection once and verify the selected photos or typed details remain available with a calm retry action.
5. Log a manual cigar smoke. Confirm the success panel offers **Log this cigar again**, **Log another**, and **Return to Vault** without requiring refresh.
6. Save disposable valuation evidence. Confirm the success panel offers **Add another valuation**, **Open cigar record**, and **Review valuation history**.
7. From a missing-record journal link, confirm the page offers both **Choose a cigar from my Vault** and **Log a cigar without changing inventory**.

## Mobile presentation

- Bottom navigation remains reachable with the keyboard open.
- Focused fields remain above the keyboard.
- Primary buttons, completion links, and record actions have comfortable touch targets.
- No horizontal page scrolling appears.
- Loading, interruption, error, and success messages remain visible and understandable.
- Back and return actions preserve the collector’s context.

## Cross-device and conflict protection

1. Save a disposable note on device one.
2. Open the same record on device two.
3. Attempt a stale edit from device two.
4. Pass only when the newer record is protected from silent replacement.

## Recovery boundary

- Inspect a controlled test export without restoring it first.
- Confirm missing, conflicting, and unchanged records are distinguished.
- Restore disposable records only, then verify the receipt and second-device result.
