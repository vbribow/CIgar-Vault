# Affiliate and Retailer Monetization Standard

**Status:** Internal implementation standard. This document does not activate an affiliate program, retailer agreement, compensated link, sponsored placement, tracking, outreach, or campaign.

## Independence

Compensation may never change organic inclusion, listing order, exact-identity matching, availability classification, normalized price, valuation evidence, reviews, Collector 25 results, Cigar Somm guidance, editorial conclusions, or correction history. Evidence URLs remain the original retailer URLs; any approved commercial parameters belong only on a separate outbound destination.

## Permitted formats

- A clearly disclosed affiliate purchase link under a separately approved retailer program.
- A separately contracted and disclosed direct referral.
- A visually separate area labeled `Paid placement` that cannot imitate an organic result.
- Aggregate retailer intelligence that contains no collector identity, inventory, preferences, history, or behavior.

Every compensated link must say: “Hojavía may receive compensation if you purchase through this link. Compensation does not change the source, evidence status, price shown, or listing order.”

## Activation gates

Every program remains inactive until the agreement, approved domains and parameters, legal and privacy review, adult and jurisdiction controls, accessible disclosure checks, financial reconciliation, emergency pause test, clean-link rollback test, and separate dated founder approval are documented.

Fox Cigars remains subject to the blanket prohibition: no outreach, tracking link, test, trial, campaign, configuration, or activation without Brian’s separate explicit approval.

## Technical and privacy behavior

- Configuration is server-only and fails closed when missing, incomplete, malformed, paused, or unapproved.
- Failed configuration produces a direct, untracked retailer link.
- Commercial decoration occurs after research and never changes result order.
- Stable evidence and duplicate detection use the original URL.
- No collector identifier, inventory identifier, email, or behavioral profile may enter an affiliate URL.
- Hojavía does not sell tobacco. Retailers remain responsible for age, identity, legality, shipping, and tax controls.

Reporting may include retailer, program, period, aggregate clicks and conversions, commissions, reversals, payment status, and compliance incidents. Do not ingest customer names, emails, addresses, order contents, or private collector record identifiers. Commercial performance must never feed organic ranking or recommendation models.

## Monitoring and rollback

Review disclosures after each relevant release and pause immediately for missing disclosure, unsafe destinations, compliance concerns, data leakage, misleading claims, or evidence contamination. Rollback removes affiliate parameters without breaking the original retailer links or evidence records.

This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Context-specific attorney review remains an activation gate.
